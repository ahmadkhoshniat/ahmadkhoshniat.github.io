# Ahmad Khoshniat

PhD Candidate in Visual and Performing Arts  
University of Texas at Dallas

Researcher | Filmmaker | Educator

I am a PhD candidate in Visual and Performing Arts at the University of Texas at Dallas. My research explores how cinematic form can generate political meaning through experimental animation and film practice. Bringing together Iranian art cinema, experimental animation, political aesthetics, and practice-as-research methodologies, I investigate the creation of political images beyond explicit representation.

Alongside my scholarly work, I am an award-winning filmmaker and animator whose films have been screened internationally in the United States, Europe, Asia, and Latin America.

Research Interests: Iranian Art Cinema, Experimental Animation, Political Aesthetics, Practice-as-Research, Intercultural Cinema, and Transnational Cinema.
